# 🚗 Vehicle Details Management System

**A centralized platform for managing vehicle information, traffic challans, service history, maintenance schedules, and overall vehicle health.**

## 📌 Overview

The **Vehicle Details Management System** is designed to simplify and organize vehicle-related information through a single, centralized platform. It helps vehicle owners and fleet managers maintain accurate vehicle records, track traffic challans, monitor service history, and keep track of vehicle health.

The system aims to reduce manual record-keeping, improve maintenance planning, provide better visibility into vehicle conditions, and make essential vehicle information easily accessible.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🚘 1. Vehicle Information Management

* Add, update, view, and manage vehicle details.
* Maintain vehicle registration numbers and identification information.
* Store vehicle make, model, manufacturing year, and fuel type.
* Maintain registration and insurance details.
* Access all vehicle information from a centralized dashboard.

### 🚨 2. Traffic Challan Management

* Maintain a record of traffic challans associated with each vehicle.
* Track challan amounts, dates, and violation details.
* Monitor pending and paid challans.
* Maintain payment dates and transaction references.
* View the complete challan history of a vehicle.

### 🔧 3. Service History Management

* Maintain a complete record of vehicle servicing.
* Record service dates, service types, and service center details.
* Track replaced parts, repairs, and maintenance expenses.
* Maintain previous service records for future reference.
* Schedule upcoming servicing based on time or mileage.

### 🩺 4. Vehicle Health Monitoring

* Monitor the overall health and maintenance condition of vehicles.
* Track engine, battery, tyre, brake, and other component conditions.
* Record vehicle inspection details and observations.
* Identify components that require attention.
* Maintain vehicle health reports based on recorded inspection data.

*Vehicle health information depends on manually entered records or supported diagnostic integrations. It is not a substitute for a professional vehicle inspection.*

### 📅 5. Maintenance & Reminders

* Track upcoming vehicle servicing.
* Monitor insurance and registration expiry dates.
* Set reminders for scheduled maintenance.
* Keep track of pending repairs and inspections.
* Reduce the risk of missing important vehicle-related deadlines.

### 📊 6. Dashboard & Reports

* View a summary of all registered vehicles.
* Track pending and paid challans.
* Review service history and maintenance expenses.
* Monitor upcoming service schedules.
* Access vehicle health and maintenance records in one place.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Centralize all vehicle-related information.
* Simplify vehicle record management.
* Improve visibility of traffic challans and payments.
* Maintain a reliable service and repair history.
* Support timely maintenance and vehicle inspections.
* Help users make informed vehicle maintenance decisions.
